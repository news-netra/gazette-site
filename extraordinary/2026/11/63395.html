PUBLIC NOTICE
IN THE MATTER OF GODREJ CONSUMER PRODUCTS BANGLADESH
LIMITED
NOTICE OF EXTRA-ORDINARY GENERAL MEETING UNDER SUB-SECTION-2
OF SECTION 296 OF THE COMPANIES ACT, 1994
This is to notify that an Extraordinary General Meeting of Godrej Consumer
Products Bangladesh Limited will be held on 30-11-2026 at its office at
Progress Tower (Level-4), House-1, Road-23, Gulshan-1, Dhaka at 4.00 PM to
transect the following special resolution with or without modification:
i.
To consider the Liquidators Statement of Financial Affairs on Members'
Voluntary Winding Up of Godrej Consumer Products Bangladesh Limited.
ii.
To Approve the Members' Voluntary Winding Up of Godrej Consumer
Products Bangladesh Limited.
FOR: Godrej Consumer Products Bangladesh Limited.
G. K. RAJBONGSHI, Advocate
Liquidators.
