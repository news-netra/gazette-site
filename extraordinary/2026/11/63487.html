NOTICE UNDER SECTION 296(2) OF THE COMPANIES ACT, 1994
FOR THE SPECIAL RESOLUTION TO VOLUNTARY WINDING UP
In the Matter of
SHANGHAI ELECTRIC POWER CONSTRUCTION CO. LTD.
At an Final Extraordinary General meeting of Shareholders of SHANGHAI
ELECTRIC POWER CONSTRUCTION CO. LTD. Duly convened and held
at the Registered office of the Company at (Bangladesh Branch): F-106,
Mohakhali School Road, Banani, Gulshan, Dhaka at 10:00 A. M. on the
18-12-2026. It has been resolved that (1) SHANGHAI ELECTRIC POWER
CONSTRUCTION CO. LTD (Reg. No. F-888) has been put into Members
voluntary winding up and (2) Mr. Sha Liangtong, Address: 68 Gaoyou Road,
Xuhui District, Shanghai City, China, has been appointed as the Liquidator of the
Company for the purpose of voluntary winding up.
(২৭০০৯)
মূল্য: টাকা ৪.০০
২৭০১০

AGENDA:
(1) Resolved that the final liquidator's report and audited accounts will be
adopted and approved.
(2) Resolved that SHANGHAI ELECTRIC POWER CONSTRUCTION
CO. LTD. will finally liquidated and the liquidator will be discharged
from 18-12-2026.
(3) Miscellaneous.
All shareholders are therefore, requested to please attend the meeting in time.
MR. SHA LIANGTONG
Liquidator
SHANGHAI ELECTRIC POWER CONSTRUCTION CO. LTD.
