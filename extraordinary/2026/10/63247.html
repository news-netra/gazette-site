NOTICE UNDER SECTION 296(2) OF THE COMPANIES ACT, 1994
FOR THE SPECIAL RESOLUTION TO VOLUNTARY WINDING UP

In the matter of
KHAN AUTO RICE MILLS LTD.

At an Final Extraordinary General Meeting of shareholders of KHAN AUTO RICE
MILLS LTD. duly convened and held at the Registered office of the Company at 73,
KAKRAIL 6th FLOOR, DHAKA at 10:00 A.M. on the 15-11-2026. It has been resolved
that (1) KHAN AUTO RICE MILLS LTD. (Reg. No. C-85780, Reg. Date: 14 JUL-10)
has been put into Members voluntary winding up and (2) MD. NASIR UDDIN KHAN,
Address: Talora Bazar, Talora, Dupchanchia, Bogra has been appointed as the
Liquidator of the Company for the purpose of voluntary winding up.

AGENDA:
(1) Resolved that the Final liquidator's report and audited accounts will be adopted and
approved.
(2) Resolved that KHAN AUTO RICE MILLS LTD. will finally liquidated and the
liquidator will be discharged from 15-11-2026.
(3) Miscellaneous.

All Shareholders are therefore, requested to please attend the meeting in time.

MD. NASIR UDDIN KHAN
Liquidator
KHAN AUTO RICE MILLS LTD.
