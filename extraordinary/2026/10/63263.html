ADVERTISEMENT OF FINAL NOTICE OF UNDER SCETION 296(2)
OF THE COMPANIES ACT, 1994 THE FINAL EXTRA ORDINARY
GENERAL MEETING TO LIQUIDATION.

A Final Notice hereby given the Final Extra-Ordinary Meeting of the LABEL
SHOP BD LIMITED, (Reg. No. C-145093, Reg. Date: 27 MAY-18) will be
held at the Registered Office at Holding No. 82/1, Beparipara, Uttarkhan,
Dhaka-1230 of the Company on the 21-11-2026 at 12.30 A.M. It has been
resolved that the LABEL SHOP BD LIMITED has been put into Member
Voluntarily Winding Up and MD. JUBAER-AL-MAHMUD, Liquidator &
Managing Director at House-31 (2nd Floor), Road 20, Sector-03, Uttara,
Dhaka-1230, Bangladesh to transact the following purpose.

AGENDA:

(1) To discuss the Final Liquidator's Report and Accounts will be adopted and
approved.

(2) To discuss of LABEL SHOP BD LIMITED will be finally liquidated and
the Liquidator will be discharged from 21st day of November, 2026.

(3) Miscellaneous (If Any).

All Shareholders are therefore, requested to please attend the meeting in time.

MD. JUBAER-AL-MAHMUD
Managing Director & Liquidator
LABEL SHOP BD LIMITED.
