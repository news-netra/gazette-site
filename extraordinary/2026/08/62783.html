NOTICE UNDER SECTION 296(2) OF THE COMPANIES ACT, 1994
FOR THE SPECIAL RESOLUTION TO VOLUNTARY WINDING UP
In the matter of
INTECOM SHOPPES LIMITED.
At an Final Extraordinary general meeting of shareholders of INTECOM SHOPPES
LIMITED duly convened and held at the Registered office of the Company at plaza A.R.,
House No. 2, Road No. 14, Sobhanbag, Dhanmondi, Dhaka-1209 at 10.00 A. M. on the
03-10-2026. It has been resolved that (1) INTECOM SHOPPES LIMITED (Reg.
No. C-202075, Reg. Date: 08-May-25) has been put into Members voluntary
winding up and (2) Mohammad Abdul Qaiyum, Managing Director at Arzu
Cottage, Road No. 5, Shantibag R/A, North Agrabad, Halishahar, Chattogram,
Bangladesh, has been appointed as the Liquidator of the Company for the purpose
of voluntary winding up.
AGENDA:
(1) Resolved that the Final liquidator's report and audited accounts will be
adopted and approved.
(2) Resolved that INTECOM SHOPPES LIMITED will finally liquidated
and the liquidator will be discharged from 03-10-2026.
(3) Miscellaneous.
All Shareholders are therefore, requested to please attend the meeting in time.
MOHAMMAD ABDUL QAIYUM
Liquidator
INTECOM SHOPPES LIMITED.
