THE INSTITUTE OF CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS OF BANGLADESH (ICAB)
NOTIFICATION
Date : 03 August 2026
No. ICAB/ENF/2026/101/01(G.N): In exercise of power conferred by clause (1)
of Article 28 of the Bangladesh Chartered Accountants Order 1973 (P.O. No.2 of
1973), the following amendments and additions to the Institute of Chartered
Accountants of Bangladesh Bye-laws, 1973 (as amended upto 31 August 2023)
are hereby made and published for general information, namely:
(1) Amendment to Bye Law 2
The Bye Law 2 (3) (bbb) shall be deleted.
(2) Amendment to Bye Law 77
The following shall be inserted after clause (2):
"(2A) The Council may constitute ICAB Public Interest Forum (IPIF) as a non-
statutory advisory body to provide strategic and forward-looking advice on
matters of public interest relevant to the accountancy profession. The President of
the Institute shall serve as the Chair and the Forum may include senior
representatives from the Government, regulatory authorities, chambers and trade
bodies, academia, policy or research institutions, and eminent persons from civil
society or other public interest sectors, as determined by the Council."
(২১৯৩৯)

(3) Amendment to Bye Law 83
The Bye Law 83 (2) (f) shall be replaced by the following:
"(f) To arrange training and examinations regularly for prospective Articled
students and recommend their course fees to the Council for approval ;"
(4) Amendment to Bye Law 100
The following shall be inserted after clause (2):
"(2A) There may be a Treasurer of each Regional Committee who shall be
elected from amongst the elected members of the Regional Committee and shall
oversee the financial affairs of the Regional Committee.”
(5) Amendment to Bye Law 106:
The Bye Law 106 shall be deleted.
(6) Amendment to Bye Law 110:
The Bye Law 110 shall be replaced by the following:
"Premium
No premium shall be Charged by the Principals from their Articled students.”
(7) Amendment to Bye Law 130
In Bye Law 130, after sub-clause (2) (b), the following shall be inserted:
"(2) (bb) Suspend, with or without monetary penalty, a member from practice as
a public accountant for providing a statutory audit service, for a period not
exceeding 05 (five) years and restrict/withheld the access to Document
Verification System (DVS) as the Council thinks fit;"
By Order of the Council
MD. QUAMRUZZAMAN
Chief Executive Officer-ICAB
