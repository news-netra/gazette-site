NOTICE
In the matter of :
Date: 04 August 2026
EARTH SCIENCE TECHNOLOGY AND POLICY SERVICES LIMITED
Notice is hereby given that the final Extraordinary General Meeting of Members
of EARTH SCIENCE TECHNOLOGY AND POLICY SERVICES
LIMITED, Reg. No. C-147747 will be held at the registered office located at
Amina Vista, Flat-5C, 30 Topkhana Road, Shahabag, Dhaka-1000, Bangladesh,
on 10 September 2026 at 10:00 A.M. for the following purposes:—
Agenda :
01. To confirm the minutes of the previous meeting;
02. To discuss about the winding-up process;
03. To receive and consider the Liquidator's report and accounts prepared by Liquidator;
04. To transact any other business that the liquidator may place before the meeting;
05. Miscellaneous (If Any).
All members are therefore requested to please attend the meeting on time.
DR. DEWAN ABDUL QUADIR
Liquidator/Managing Director.
