"316. Religion counselling officers proceeding to their homes on

"317. Religion counselling officers moving on duty. Subject to
all rules of general applicability, religion counselling officer
is entitled to conveyance when travelling on duty. When a
religion counselling officer moves in relief on permanent
duty, his family, if permitted to accompany or follow him, is
entitled to conveyance from the station whence he moved or
from any other station to his new station provided no
expense is thereby caused to the State."।
