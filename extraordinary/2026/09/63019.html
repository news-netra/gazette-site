NOTICE UNDER SECTION 289 OF THE COMPANIES ACT 1994
FOR RESOLUTION TO WINDING UP VOLUNTARILY

IN THE MATTER OF
PIXEL AV & IT SOLUTION LTD.

An extraordinary general meeting of the shareholders of PIXEL AV & IT
SOLUTION LTD. duly convened and held at the Registered Office of the
Company in Dhaka. It has been resolved that PIXEL AV & IT SOLUTION
LTD., Reg. No.C-183894, Reg. Date: 18 Sep-2022 has been put into Members
for voluntary Winding up on 10-09-2026, SHEIKH ALAMGIR, Managing
Director/Liquidator Address: House No. 11, Road No. 10, Lane-05, Section-11,
Block-C, Mirpur, Dhaka has been appointed as the Liquidator of the Company.

All concerned are therefore, requested to contact henceforth with the Liquidator
in respect of any affairs of the company.

SHEIKH ALAMGIR
Managing Director/Liquidator
PIXEL AV & IT SOLUTION LTD.
